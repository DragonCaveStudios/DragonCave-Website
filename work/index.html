I produce tracks for other artists and write music for indie games. You bring the idea; I'll help you build it, from first sketch to finished track.

What I offer

Production for artists

Dance, house, electronic and pop.

Game music

Looping tracks, menu themes and full soundtracks, made by someone who builds games too.

How it will work

  1. Fill in the enquiry form with your idea, links to reference tracks and your timeline.
  2. I reply within a few days with questions and a rough plan.
  3. We agree the details in writing: what's being made, credits, payment and any revenue split.
  4. I make it, share drafts, and we refine it together.
  5. You get the final files, ready to release or drop into your game.
