About

Fierce, loyal, and always making something.

DragonCave is a one-person studio in the UK, making indie games as DragonCave Studios and upbeat dance music as DragonCave.

Dragons have been my thing for as long as I can remember. I see them as fierce and loyal, which is how I'd describe myself, so the name chose itself. I started out on YouTube as ADragonGaming in 2015, and became DragonCave Studios in 2017, when game development got serious.

The games go back much further, though. My first computer was an Amstrad CPC 464, a birthday present in 1990, and I spent the next few years trying to make games on it. Back then, making games properly was a hobby I couldn't afford, so I put it aside. When Epic Games made Unreal Engine free to use, I picked it straight back up and taught myself Blueprint scripting. Today every DragonCave game is made in Godot 4. One day, I might even build my own engine.

Music has always been there too. At school, my party trick was listening to that week's new releases and predicting where they'd chart, and I got most of them right. Then the pandemic made me stop and ask what I'd leave behind if I disappeared tomorrow. The honest answer was "not much", and my creativity went into overdrive. Between 2022 and 2026, I made around 85 tracks, enough for eight albums, alongside two games that are nearly finished and two more in the pipeline.

What I love about both is that you start with nothing, and what you end up with depends entirely on how creative you are. I have Asperger's, and it's a big part of why I can be so creative.

My influences are all over the map. In games, it's the GTA series, Battlefield and football management games. In music, anyone listening will hear Daft Punk straight away, but my tastes run from Bach and Mozart to The Beatles, New Order, Tupac and Gorillaz.

The first DragonCave Studios game arrives in 2027, and a new DragonCave album lands every summer.
