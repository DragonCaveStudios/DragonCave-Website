Privacy

Plain English, no small print.

This site doesn't use tracking cookies or advertising.

Mailing list: if you sign up, your email address is stored with our mailing list provider and used only to send the news you chose. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Contact forms: messages go to our inbox through a form service and are used only to reply to you.

Videos and players: YouTube and other players load only when you press play. Their own privacy policies apply from then.

Fonts: the fonts on this site are stored on this site, so loading a page doesn't contact any other company.

The mailing list and contact forms aren't open yet. This page will name the services used when they are.

Questions, or want your details removed? Email hello@dragoncavestudios.co.uk.
